# KaziOS Video Tutorial — Ready-to-Read Scripts

**Rule:** One video = one job. Under 3 minutes each. New shop owner watches Video 1 + 2 on day one; the rest they open only when they need it. This is why it stops feeling impossible.

**Recording setup (30 seconds, no gear needed):**
- Easiest: **Loom** (free) — records screen + your voice, gives you a link to send on WhatsApp.
- Or phone screen recorder + talk over it.
- Record in **simple English or Kiswahili** (or both versions). Move the mouse slowly. Don't edit out pauses — real beats polished.
- Say what you're about to click BEFORE you click it.

---

## VIDEO 1 — "Your first sale" (THE one that matters)

*Goal: a brand-new cashier makes a sale start to finish. Keep it to just Cash first.*

1. **(Login screen)** "Open KaziOS, type your email and password, tap Sign in."
2. **(Dashboard)** "This is your home. To sell, tap **POS** on the left."
3. **(POS opens)** "Your till is ready. If you have more than one branch or store, pick it here — most shops leave it as Main."
4. **Add a product — do it two ways:**
   - "Type the name or scan the barcode..." *(do it)*
   - "...or just tap the product here to add it."
5. **(Current Sale fills up)** "It's added to the sale. Tap **+** to add more of the same, or **–** to remove one."
6. **(Customer)** "For a walk-in, leave it as Walk-in. If you know the customer, search their name or phone so their history is saved. Most sales: just skip it."
7. **(Payment method)** "Choose how they're paying. For cash, it's already on **Cash**."
8. **(Cash tendered)** "Type the money they gave you. See — it shows the **change** to give back automatically."
9. **Tap "Charge".** "Done. Here's the receipt — the sale is complete."
10. "Tap **Start new sale** and you're ready for the next customer."

*End card: "That's it — that's a sale. Next video: taking a card payment."*

## VIDEO 2 — "Taking a card payment"

*Goal: show the card popup AND the trust point — money goes to their bank.*

1. "Build the sale the same way. This time choose **Card**."
2. "Type the customer's email — they need it for the receipt."
3. "Tap **Charge**. A secure Paystack window opens — the customer types their card there. Your shop never sees the card number."
4. "When they pay, the sale flips to **Paid** on its own. You can't fake it — the system checks with the bank."
5. **(The selling moment)** "And here's the best part: that money goes **straight to your own bank account** — 98% is yours. We only keep 2%. Your money never sits with us."
6. "If the card is declined, the sale stays unpaid and the stock is safe. Just take payment another way."

---

## VIDEO 3 — "Add a product & see your stock"

1. "Tap **Products**, then **New**."
2. "Give it a name, a code, a price, and how many you have in stock. Save."
3. "Now it's in your till — search it and sell it."
4. "Watch the stock go down each time you sell."
5. "When stock gets low, KaziOS flags it so you reorder before you run out."

---

## VIDEO 4 — "Receiving new stock from a supplier"

1. "When new goods arrive, go to **Purchasing** and record what came in."
2. "Your stock goes up automatically — no double counting."
3. "Now your shelf number and your system always match."

---

## VIDEO 5 — "See how your business did today"

1. "Tap **Reports**."
2. "Here's what you sold today, what made money, and what's running low."
3. "Pick a date range to compare days."
4. "This is your business at a glance — no notebook."

---

## VIDEO 6 — "Set up so card money goes to your bank"

*Goal: the trust/closer video for a hesitant owner.*

1. "Go to **Settings**, then the billing/payments area."
2. "Add your Paystack subaccount — your own bank details."
3. "From now on, every card sale splits: your bank gets your share directly."

## How to deliver them

- **Day 1 to a new shop:** send Video 1 + Video 2 only. Nothing else. Overwhelming a new user loses them.
- Put the rest on a simple page/playlist and say "the others are here when you need them."
- Keep a **1-page cheat sheet** by the till: *log in → POS → tap product → choose payment → Charge.*

## Common filming mistakes to avoid

- ❌ Teaching everything in one 20-minute video → nobody finishes it.
- ❌ Fast clicking and silence → they can't follow.
- ❌ Assuming they know "POS," "SKU," "inventory" → say "your till," "product code," "your stock."
- ✅ One job, slow, out loud, under 3 minutes.
