# KaziOS — Till Cheat Sheet

*Print this. Tape it next to the register. No training needed.*

---

## Make a sale (the everyday job)

```
1.  Sign in  (email + password)
2.  Tap  POS
3.  Tap the product  →  it goes into the sale
      +  add another        –  remove one
4.  Choose Payment method
      Cash    → type money given → shows the CHANGE
      Card    → type customer email → Charge
      M-Pesa  → Charge → customer enters PIN
5.  Tap  Charge
6.  Done → receipt shows →  Start new sale
```

---

## The 3 rules that keep you out of trouble

- **Card / M-Pesa sale = "Paid" only after the customer pays.**
  If they cancel or it fails, the sale stays **unpaid**. No harm done — just take cash instead.

- **Walk-in customer?** Leave it as Walk-in. Only attach a customer if you know them.

- **Wrong thing added?** Tap **–** on the line, or **Clear** to empty the whole sale.

## What the buttons mean (plain words)

| Word on screen | What it really is |
|---|---|
| **POS** | Your till — where you sell |
| **Product** | Something you sell |
| **Stock / Inventory** | How many you have on the shelf |
| **Invoice** | The record of a sale |
| **Reports** | How your business did |
| **Settings** | Your shop details, bank, users |

---

## Stuck? (the 4 things that cover 90% of problems)

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Can't sign in | Check email + password; tap **Forgot password** |
| Card won't open | Allow pop-ups for this site, then retry |
| Product not found | **Products → New** to add it |
| Stock looks wrong | Count the shelf, then fix it in **Products** |
